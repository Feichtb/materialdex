Layer 01 — Interior Paint

"Zero-VOC" isn't zero.

The label measures what evaporates during a standardized test — not what migrates into your air for years. Manufacturers shifted from regulated volatile organic compounds to unregulated semi-volatile organic compounds. The EPA itself warns that VOC labels "may not properly assess all of the VOCs emitted from the product."

Layer 02 — Gypsum Board

The most boring material with the biggest national-scale impact.

The US consumes approximately 28 billion square feet of gypsum wallboard annually. Drying energy accounts for 68–81% of drywall's total carbon footprint. A 22% weight reduction in lightweight panels directly translates to less kiln energy — and at 28 billion square feet, small percentages become megatons.

Layer 03 — Insulation

The blowing agent that outweighs the building.

Legacy XPS insulation's blowing agent — HFC-134a, the gas trapped inside the foam board at manufacturing — has a global warming potential 1,430× that of CO₂. Over a building's 75-year life, nearly half the climate damage from XPS comes not from making it, but from the gas slowly leaking out of it.

Quiz — Reveal your assumptions

You're insulating a roof. How much more global warming impact does traditional XPS rigid foam have compared to mineral wool at the same R-value?

40× or more. Legacy XPS (Owens Corning FOAMULAR): 57.8 kg CO₂e/m² at RSI-1 over 75 years. ROCKWOOL Comfortbatt: 1.07–1.4 kg CO₂e/m². Of the XPS total, 28.5 kg CO₂e — 49% — is HFC-134a diffusing out of the foam during the use phase. The gas was GWP 1,430× CO₂.

Caveat: XPS provides superior moisture resistance below grade. Mineral wool and cellulose are not always direct substitutes. The next generation HFO-based XPS (FOAMULAR NGX) reduces GWP ~80% — but still runs 2× higher than mineral wool.

A 2019 study (Bello et al., Int. J. Hygiene & Environmental Health) found 16% of spray foam applicator breathing-zone samples exceeded the NIOSH limit for MDI. The EPA recommends 24–72 hours of vacancy after installation. No standardized HPD exists for most closed-cell spray foams.

Layer 05 — Air / Weather Barrier

The transparency gap.

This is the thinnest, most invisible layer — and the one with the least disclosed chemistry. Fewer than 5% of construction adhesives and sealants have Health Product Declarations. You know more about your breakfast cereal than about what's bonding the envelope of a building its occupants will breathe near for 50 years.

Layer 06 — Exterior Cladding

The replacement cycle changes everything.

Brick lasts 150+ years. Vinyl siding lasts 20–40 years in practice, regardless of what the industry EPD claims. Over a century, the "affordable" option generates more total carbon and more landfill waste. The upfront cost is only half the story.

LMN Architects found up to a 58% carbon difference between precast and thin-brick-on-metal-stud wall systems — same wall, radically different lifecycle.

The vinyl siding industry EPD assumes a 50-year service life. Independent sources consistently cite 20–40 years before fading, brittleness, and UV degradation. PVC is Red Listed by the International Living Future Institute. Each replacement cycle sends non-recyclable material to landfill.

The pattern

After five layers, the same thing.

Data was available. It was buried in EPDs, HPDs, and Declare labels that nobody pulled into the design process. These aren't obscure edge cases — they're the five materials on every mid-rise project in the country.

"The blowing agent in a single layer of legacy XPS insulation can carry more global warming potential than all other building materials in the wall assembly combined."

KPMB Lab · Embodied Carbon in Common Insulation Materials · May 2021

"Over 430 workers in one US state have contracted an incurable lung disease from a countertop material that 93% of specifiers choose without knowing its silica content."

Cal/OSHA 2019–2025 · Rose et al., MMWR 2019

"A 'zero-VOC' paint label is tested using a method the EPA itself says 'may not properly assess all of the VOCs emitted from the product.'"

US EPA Technical Overview of VOC Emissions

Tools for transparent material comparison exist — EC3, HPD Repository, Declare, Pharos. Specifying without consulting them is the architectural equivalent of prescribing medicine without reading the label.
