Materialdex — Sustainable Materials Suggestor

Version 2.0.0  ·  Revit 2025 and 2026  ·  ben.materialdex@gmail.com

Overview

Materialdex is an AI-assisted tool that helps architects find sustainable product alternatives for the materials in their Revit model. For each material it surfaces real manufacturer products with links to EPDs, HPDs, Declare labels, and VOC certifications.

Free Scan Limit

Materialdex includes 50 free scans per device. After that, add your own Perplexity API key in the app settings (≈ $0.08 per scan). Your key is never stored — it is sent only when you initiate a scan.

Getting Started

1. Open a Revit project.

2. Click Show Materialdex in the Materialdex ribbon tab to open the panel.

3. Click Extract Materials to read materials from the active model. Materials and their quantities appear in the panel.

4. Select a material from the list and click Find Products.

5. Review the product recommendations. Expand a product card to see its documentation links (EPD, HPD, Declare, VOC).

6. Bookmark products you want to keep. They are saved to your Library tab.

7. Re-run scans at any time as the model evolves.

Installation

Step 1: Download the latest Materialdex-v2.0.0.zip from github.com/Feichtb/materialdex/releases.

Step 2: Extract the ZIP and locate the Materialdex.bundle folder.

Step 3: Copy the Materialdex.bundle folder to one of these locations:

  • Per-user (recommended): %AppData%\Autodesk\ApplicationPlugins\
  • Per-machine (admin required): %ProgramData%\Autodesk\ApplicationPlugins\

Step 4: Launch Revit. The Materialdex tab will appear in the ribbon.

Uninstallation

1. Close Revit.

2. Delete the Materialdex.bundle folder from the ApplicationPlugins location where you installed it.

3. Restart Revit.

Verifying Product Documentation

Important: Always verify documentation links before using them in specifications. AI-generated results are a starting point, not a substitute for professional judgment.

  • Expiration dates: EPDs, HPDs, and certifications expire. Confirm documents are current.
  • Regional relevance: Documentation may apply to specific regions. Verify it applies to your project location.
  • Product variants: Links may reference a related product in the same line. Confirm the exact model matches your specification.
  • Manufacturer updates: Check the manufacturer's website for the most recent documentation.

Additional Information

Data Usage

  • Material names from your open Revit model are sent to the Materialdex web service to generate suggestions.
  • Project ZIP code and goals (if entered) are also included to improve regional relevance.
  • Model geometry and client information are not collected.
  • If you use your own Perplexity API key, your key is sent per-request and not stored.

Known Limitations

  • Results are based on material names and typical use cases. Generic or abbreviated names may yield fewer or less relevant results.
  • Documentation links can occasionally point to outdated or region-specific documents. Always verify before specifying.
  • The plugin requires an active internet connection.

Troubleshooting

Plugin tab not appearing in Revit

  • Confirm the Materialdex.bundle folder is directly inside ApplicationPlugins\, not nested in a subfolder.
  • Check Manage → Add-ins for error messages.
  • Restart Revit after installation.

WebView2 Runtime error

Download and install WebView2 from: developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2

Cannot connect / panel is blank

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Check that your firewall allows Revit to reach materialdex.netlify.app.
  • Use the refresh button in the panel header.

Materials not extracting

  • Ensure a Revit project file (.rvt) is open and active.
  • Try closing and reopening the Materialdex panel.
